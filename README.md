# My AI Automation Interview Coach

Personal Codex skill for AI automation, RAG/LLM applications, Power Platform, governance/security, full-stack AI, and Sweden-localized interview preparation.

## Privacy

`references/profile.md` and `logs/` are intentionally ignored by Git.

- Use `references/profile.example.md` as a template.
- Keep your real resume facts in local `references/profile.md`.
- Keep mock scorecards and session history in local `logs/`.

## Main Capabilities

- Strict 1-4 calibrated mock interviews
- Project deep-dive ladders
- RAG/LLM system design practice
- Power Platform and Copilot Studio interview prep
- Governance/security interview prep
- Full-stack AI app interviews
- Coding/debugging rounds
- Resume/JD alignment
- Sweden/Stockholm interview strategy
- Take-home and project walkthrough strategy
- Weakness logs and session continuity
