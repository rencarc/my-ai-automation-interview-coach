# Reverse Questions For Interviewers

Use this for the final 5-10 minutes of interviews.

## Principles

Good reverse questions should:

- show judgment,
- reveal team reality,
- be specific to role/company,
- not sound confrontational,
- help the user decide whether the company is healthy.

## For Hiring Manager

- "What would make someone successful in this role in the first six months?"
- "Where is the team currently using AI automation, and where has adoption been harder than expected?"
- "What are the main tradeoffs between low-code and custom-code solutions in your current environment?"
- "What are the most important technical risks for this role?"
- "If someone struggled in this role, what would usually be the reason?"

## For Future Teammates

- "How are technical decisions usually made on the team?"
- "What does a good code review or solution review look like here?"
- "How much ownership does a developer have from requirement to production?"
- "What parts of the workflow are currently painful or manual?"
- "How does the team handle incidents or failed automations?"

## For HR / Recruiter

- "What are the next steps and expected timeline?"
- "How is success evaluated during probation or the first six months?"
- "Is this role mainly internal-product focused, client-facing, or mixed?"
- "What is the expected office/remote setup?"

## Sweden-Specific Wording

Avoid blunt wording like:

- "Do you work overtime a lot?"

Use:

- "How does the team usually handle periods with high workload?"
- "What does sustainable delivery look like on this team?"

Avoid:

- "Is the manager good?"

Use:

- "How does feedback usually work between the manager and the team?"

## Red Flags To Listen For

- no clear success criteria,
- no ownership boundaries,
- "we are like a family" used to justify overwork,
- no time for onboarding,
- AI adoption without governance,
- unclear data-access rules,
- high consultant/client churn without explanation.
