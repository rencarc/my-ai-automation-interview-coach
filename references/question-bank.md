# Question Bank And Practice Plan

Use this to generate targeted question sets or a preparation schedule.

2. How do you decide whether to use Copilot Studio, Power Automate, or a custom app?
3. How would you connect a website form to an internal case workflow?
4. What makes an AI automation workflow unsafe?
5. How do you handle low-confidence AI output?
6. How do you measure the success of workflow automation?
7. How do you explain automation tradeoffs to non-technical stakeholders?
8. How would you redesign a manual onboarding process using Power Platform?
9. How do you prevent duplicate workflow executions?
10. What would you log for a workflow action proposed by AI?

## Four-Week Practice Plan

### Week 1: Positioning And Stories

- Polish "tell me about yourself".
- Build 8 STAR stories from the story bank.
- Review resume/JD alignment for 3 target roles.
- Do 2 behavioral mocks.

### Week 2: RAG And AI Automation

- Do 2 RAG system design mocks.
- Drill fallback, citations, permission filtering, and evaluation.
- Generate one question package for an internal RAG assistant.

### Week 3: Power Platform And Full-stack

- Do 1 Power Platform solution walkthrough.
- Do 1 full-stack AI architecture walkthrough.
- Practice 5 coding/debugging tasks from `coding-debugging-interview.md`.

### Week 4: Mixed Mock Loop

- Run one full loop:
  - behavioral,
  - RAG design,
  - Power Platform case,
  - coding/API task.
- Create a final risk list and rehearse concise answers.

## Final Readiness Checklist

- Can explain each major project in under 2 minutes.
- Can draw FlowPilot AI and Gote Note architecture.
- Can explain RAG fallback and evaluation.
- Can explain RBAC/RLS/audit logs clearly.
- Can answer why Power Platform vs custom code.
- Has 8 behavioral stories with concrete outcomes.
- Has 3 role-specific questions to ask interviewers.
