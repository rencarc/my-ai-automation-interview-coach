# Sweden / Stockholm Interview Strategy

Use this for Sweden-localized interview preparation.

## Market Positioning

For Stockholm, position the user as:

> AI automation and full-stack developer with Microsoft Power Platform, RAG, and security-aware workflow experience, plus business operations background for stakeholder-facing automation work.

This fits especially well with:

- Microsoft consulting firms,
- digital transformation consultancies,
- internal business systems teams,
- AI automation teams,
- public-sector or regulated workflow environments,
- startups building AI workflow tools.

## Consulting vs Product Company

### Consulting Firms

Likely examples: Fellowmind, Sopra Steria, Knowit, CGI, Devoteam, Columbus-style Microsoft partners, and similar firms.

They often test:

- client communication,
- structured problem solving,
- Power Platform / Microsoft stack knowledge,
- ability to explain tradeoffs simply,
- adaptability across clients,
- Swedish workplace collaboration style.

Good positioning:

- "I can bridge business process and technical implementation."
- "I am comfortable with stakeholders and requirements discovery."
- "I can work across low-code and custom-code solutions."

### Product Companies

They often test:

- product engineering depth,
- full-stack implementation,
- system design,
- ownership,
- maintainability,
- scaling and monitoring.

Good positioning:

- "I build end-to-end AI applications with attention to auth, data boundaries, fallback, and operations."

## Typical Sweden Interview Flow

Common sequence:

1. HR/recruiter screen
2. Hiring manager conversation
3. Technical interview or case
4. Take-home assignment or live technical discussion
5. Culture/team fit
6. Sometimes client-facing interview for consulting roles
7. References

## Communication Style

Swedish interviews often reward:

- calm confidence,
- collaboration,
- practical examples,
- honesty about what you know and don't know,
- low ego,
- autonomy without bragging.

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims,
- sounding like you did everything alone,
- aggressive self-promotion,
- pretending to know something.

Use wording like:

- "My contribution was..."
- "The tradeoff we considered was..."
- "I would validate that by..."
- "I have not used that exact tool in production, but I have worked with a similar pattern..."

## Work Permit / Relocation Questions

Prepare a concise, factual answer. Do not over-explain unless asked.

Template:

```text
I am currently based in Stockholm and completed my master's studies in Sweden. I am looking for roles in Sweden and can discuss the practical work-permit process clearly if needed.
```

Customize with the user's actual current status before using.

## Salary Expectation

Swedish employers often ask expected monthly salary.

Preparation rule:

- Do market research before interviews.
- Give a range in SEK/month gross.
- Anchor on role level, city, and total package.
- Avoid giving a number before understanding scope if possible.

Template:

```text
Based on the role scope and Stockholm market, I am thinking in the range of [X-Y] SEK per month, but I am flexible depending on responsibilities, learning opportunity, and total package.
```

The skill should not invent a salary number; ask the user for target roles and current market data first.

## Culture Fit Questions

Likely questions:

- How do you work in a team?
- How do you handle ambiguity?
- Tell me about a conflict.
- How do you prefer to receive feedback?
- How do you work with non-technical stakeholders?
- Why Sweden / why Stockholm?

Strong answer style:

- concrete,
- humble,
- collaborative,
- outcome-oriented,
- not over-polished.
