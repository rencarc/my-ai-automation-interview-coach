# Sweden Offer And Negotiation

Use this after salary expectations, verbal offers, or written offers in Sweden.

## Do Not Invent Numbers

Ask the user for:

- role title,
- seniority,
- city,
- company type,
- current offer,
- benefits,
- work permit constraints,
- competing processes,
- current market research.

## Components To Review

- monthly gross salary in SEK,
- probation period,
- vacation days / semester,
- occupational pension / tjanstepension,
- wellness allowance / friskvardsbidrag,
- remote/hybrid policy,
- working hours and overtime expectations,
- competence development budget,
- equipment,
- bonus or variable compensation,
- notice period,
- relocation or permit support if relevant.

## Negotiation Style In Sweden

Use calm, collaborative language. Avoid aggressive pressure.

Template:

```text
Thank you, I am very interested in the role. Based on the scope we discussed and my experience with AI automation, Power Platform, and RAG applications, I was hoping we could discuss whether there is flexibility around [salary/benefit]. I am thinking of [range/request], but I am open to finding a package that works for both sides.
```

## Multiple Processes

If the user has another process:

```text
I wanted to be transparent that I am also in another process, but this role is very interesting to me. Could you share the expected timeline for next steps?
```

## Offer Comparison

Compare:

- learning curve,
- tech stack match,
- manager quality,
- visa/work permit support,
- stability,
- salary and benefits,
- commute/remote,
- long-term role narrative.
