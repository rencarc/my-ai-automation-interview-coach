# AI Automation Interview

Use this for roles involving workflow automation, business process improvement, AI agents, API integrations, and internal knowledge workflows.

## Positioning

The user's strongest positioning:

> Applied AI and automation developer who combines business-process understanding with hands-on implementation in Copilot Studio, Power Platform, RAG, APIs, and secure workflow design.

Emphasize the bridge between:

- business operations experience,
- stakeholder-facing process analysis,
- workflow automation,
- AI assistant implementation,
- security-aware access and escalation design.

## Likely Interview Themes

- How to identify a process worth automating.
- How to turn unstructured requests into structured cases.
- When to use Copilot Studio vs custom full-stack AI app.
- How to integrate website/API inputs with internal workflows.
- How to design fallback and manual escalation.
- How to handle permissions, sensitive data, and auditability.
- How to explain automation value to non-technical stakeholders.

## Mock Questions

1. Tell me about an automation workflow you designed end to end.
2. How do you decide whether a process should use a chatbot, a form, or a workflow automation?
3. How would you connect an external website form to an internal Copilot/Power Platform workflow?
4. What can go wrong in an AI workflow, and how would you handle it?
5. How do you design fallback when an AI assistant is not confident?
6. How do you make internal knowledge easier for an AI assistant to retrieve?
7. How would you measure whether an automation project is successful?
8. Tell me about a time your business background helped you build a better technical solution.

## Evaluation Bar

Strong answers should include:

- the business problem,
- the users and stakeholders,
- the workflow before/after,
- systems involved,
- data boundaries,
- failure handling,
- security/permission model,
- measurable or observable impact.

Weak answers only list tools without explaining process, tradeoffs, or business outcome.
